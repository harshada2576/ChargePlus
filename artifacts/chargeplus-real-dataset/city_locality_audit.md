# ChargePlus Dataset — Geographic Normalization & Town/Locality Audit (48 Records)

## 1. Executive Summary

This document audits all **48 charging stations** in `chargeplus-real-dataset` where the normalized `city` field differs from the raw Open Charge Map `Town` attribute.

### Geographic Principles Applied
1. **Zero Data Loss**: The original raw `Town` value is 100% retained and promoted to the `locality` field whenever it provides more granular neighborhood, suburb, or highway waypoint information.
2. **Distinction between City, Locality, and District**:
   - **`city`**: Represents the primary municipal corporation, urban aggregation, or district nodal commercial hub (e.g. Pune, Satara, Kolhapur, New Delhi, Hubballi, Mysuru).
   - **`locality`**: Represents the specific neighborhood, village, industrial park, metro station, or highway corridor landmark (e.g. Baner, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Aerocity, Shirwal, Wele, Surur, Panambur).
   - **`district`**: Reflected by postal code prefix, state bounds, and primary administrative headquarters.
3. **No Geographic Invention**: Every normalized assignment is backed by official PIN-code routing, municipal boundaries, highway waypoint coordinates, or verified government renaming (e.g. Hubli -> Hubballi).
